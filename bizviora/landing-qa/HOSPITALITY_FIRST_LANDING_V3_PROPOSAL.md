## Page hierarchy
1 Hero: actual guest-time-money pains; neutral honest availability note.
2 "Một hành trình khách — nhiều doanh thu có thể bỏ lỡ": Discovery→Inquiry→Direct booking→Before arrival→At stay→At café→After checkout, with measurable gaps.
3 Six job-to-be-done cards:
- AI Customer / multilingual inquiries with approved handoff (speed, team hours).
- Direct Booking conversion and OTA commission analysis (direct share, net room revenue).
- Guest Assistant for check-in, transport and tour questions (inquiry volume/resolution).
- Upsell/Cross-sell homestay→Cozy Garden→tour→rental (attach rate, average spend/guest).
- AI Marketing with attribution / consent-based remarketing (qualified lead and booking CAC).
- Daily owner's commercial dashboard: bookings, room nights, extras, cost, net revenue; private finance segregated (contribution, labor hours).
For each show tags: prototype / under development / E2E verified from actual evidence, NEVER label "working" by plan alone.
4 Segmented sections: Homestay/Small hotel and Cafe/Restaurant, each with tailored examples and CTA.
5 Real workflow diagram using synthetic data and prominent "MÔ PHỎNG" marker. No guest identity, price, or performance claims without evidence.
6 Owner story: Lavender/Cozy as pilot *only if factual*. Name business connection, don't invent results.
7 ROI model with user-editable assumptions: OTA commission saved = verified direct bookings * comparable OTA commission; Upsell = attach rate * item contribution; hours saved = verified automations * time; report projections as scenarios, not outcomes.
8 Consent-based discovery form: business type, international guest share (optional), top pain, contact, consent; send only after secure backend Sheets E2E verified.
9 FAQ about integrations, language, booking/OTA, onboarding, data isolation, rollout and pricing (no unsupported claims).
